Product

One workspace from analysis code to signed document.

Writers, statisticians, reviewers and agents work from the same verified definitions and values. When the analysis changes, every document shows which numbers moved.

Tour

What your team sees

Project › Evidence

The cohort, read from the code

Each inclusion and exclusion step shows the patients left after it and links to the lines that apply it. Analysts verify each definition, and the verification goes stale when the code changes. CDISC ADaM names read in plain English.

Project › Manuscript

Tracked changes, every number linked

Writers, co-authors and agents work in one draft, and a rerun updates the numbers and shows what moved. The number audit finds numbers typed by hand. Export to Word with real tracked changes, or import a co-author’s edits as suggestions.

Workspace › House style

Your house style, learned from past papers

Upload past manuscripts and abstracts. Silogram learns spelling, voice, sentence length, how you write P values, CIs and n, preferred terms and abbreviations. Every draft and every agent follows it, and the house-style check proposes mechanical fixes as tracked suggestions.

Settings › AI & agents

Agents suggest. People decide.

The writing assistant runs on the model your company has approved. Outside agents connect over MCP or the REST API with scoped, revocable tokens. They read verified context, and their edits arrive as tracked suggestions under their own name, with numbers linked or flagged and every call logged for disclosure.

Portfolio · Project › Access

Forecasts tied to the decisions they unlock

Every document shows a most-likely and a 90% date, recalculated from the work done so far. Link it to the coverage, formulary, guideline or approval decision it supports, and the portfolio shows each decision’s likely date and what a week of delay costs.

More on speed to access

Project › Journal & checklist

Journal requirements, each with its source

Every requirement quotes the author guidelines or says Not verified, and the readiness check compares your draft with each one. Reporting checklists cover STROBE, RECORD, CONSORT 2025, STARD 2015 and TRIPOD+AI.

Project › Sign-off

Signed, locked, and on the record

Signatures record the signer, the time and the meaning, bound to a digest of the exact text signed. Approval locks the document until someone reopens it with a reason, and the audit trail shows every change before and after.
