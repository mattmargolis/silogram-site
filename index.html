How it works

From the analysis repository to a signed document

  1. Step 1

    Connect the analysis repository

    Point Silogram at the Git repository that holds your SQL, R and Python. GitHub, GitLab and Bitbucket work with a read-only token.

    northwind/nw-201-analysis · main · 4f2a9c1

  2. Step 2

    Read the code in plain language

    Each cohort step shows the patients left after it, and each variable and endpoint links to its lines and commit.

    SAFFL == "Y" → in the safety populationCNSR == 0 → event observed, not censored

  3. Step 3

    Draft with every number linked

    Writers and approved agents draft with tracked changes, in your house style. Each number links to an analysis value or gets flagged.

    {{hr_rfs}} renders as 0.88 and links to analysis/survival.R:118

  4. Step 4

    Review, sign and submit

    Co-authors suggest, writers decide, approvers sign. The Word export keeps the tracked changes, and the access plan shows what the date means.

    NW-201_primary-results_v6.docx · signed · locked

Tour the product

Agentic, with people in charge

Agents draft. People decide.

Bring the models and agents your company has approved. They read verified definitions, values and your house style, and every edit they make arrives as a tracked suggestion under their own name.

  • Any approved model

    The writing assistant runs on your company’s model keys. Writers choose the model for each run.

  • Any agent, over MCP or REST

    Scoped, revocable tokens let outside agents read context, propose edits and comment. Nothing they write reaches the text until a person accepts it.

  • Logged for disclosure

    Every model and agent call is recorded, and the use-of-AI section is drafted from that log.
