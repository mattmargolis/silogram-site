Security and compliance

Built for regulated work.

Silogram is designed to support 21 CFR Part 11 and HIPAA requirements for electronic records, electronic signatures and protected health information. Validation in your environment and your SOPs stay with you; Silogram supplies the controls and the evidence.

Records and signatures

Every change on the record

  • Audit trail

    Append-only and tamper-evident. The database records who changed what and when, with the old and new values, and refuses changes that don’t name who made them.

  • Electronic signatures

    Each signature records the signer, the date and time, and its meaning, with password re-entry, bound to a SHA-256 digest of the exact content signed.

  • Approval locks

    Approval needs a review by someone else and no open suggestions. It locks the document until someone reopens it with a reason.

  • Record package

    Export a document’s signatures, history and audit entries, or the workspace audit trail as CSV or JSON, for an inspection or your own archive.

Access and data

The right people, the right data

  • Two-factor sign-in and lockout

    Authenticator-app codes, which admins can require for everyone. Repeated failed sign-in or signing attempts lock the account.

  • Automatic sign-out

    Idle sessions end after a time the workspace sets, checked in the browser and again on the server.

  • Roles

    Owners, admins, analysts, writers, reviewers and viewers, each with only the permissions the role needs.

  • PHI guard and BAA-covered models

    No-PHI workspaces keep likely patient identifiers out of uploads, model calls and agent writes. PHI workspaces allow only model connections and agents covered by a business associate agreement.

  • Read-only to your code

    Silogram reads your analysis repository with a read-only token and never writes to it.

  • Encryption

    Model keys and repository tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, agent tokens are stored as hashes, and connections use TLS.

  • No training on your documents

    Silogram never trains AI models on your documents, and your house style stays in your workspace.

Validation

Evidence for your validation, generated from the tests

Each Part 11 and HIPAA requirement has an ID. Automated tests that verify it carry that ID, and every release produces a traceability matrix with the test evidence.

Shared responsibility. Silogram supplies the technical controls, the requirement list, the traceability matrix and test evidence, and validation templates. Your organization keeps its SOPs, training records, user acceptance testing in your environment, and its own HIPAA program.
