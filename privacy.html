Privacy

This notice covers this website. The Silogram product is covered by each customer's agreement.

What the website collects

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is a set of static pages: nothing you do on them is recorded by Silogram.

Services the pages use

Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address and browser details to Google when a page opens (Google's privacy policy). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security (GitHub's privacy statement).

Email

If you email us, we receive your address and message and use them only to reply and to talk with you about Silogram. We don't sell or share them.
